Providers deliver webhooks more than once, out of order, late, and again after a timeout. A handler that passes one manual test can still create a second order on a duplicate, accept a body nobody signed, or leave state wrong when events arrive in reverse.

Webhook Trap captures real deliveries from Stripe, GitHub, Shopify and any Standard Webhooks sender, then replays them against your handler in nine scenarios. A small agent on your machine sends the deliveries to localhost, signs them with a secret that never leaves your machine, and reads your database state between deliveries. The result is a list of findings with the evidence, the cause when the run shows it, and a fix.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Capture">
    Point a provider at your capture URL. Every request is stored as received: method, path, headers and the exact body bytes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prove">
    Select captured events and start a run. Your paired agent sends them to your handler as Once, Twice, Shuffled, Late, Retry after error, Tampered, Unsigned, Burst and Slow budget.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the verdict">
    Each finding names the scenario, the deliveries that broke the rule, what happened, why it matters for that provider, and how to fix it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep it in CI">
    `webhooktrap test` runs the same scenarios from fixture files in your repository and exits non-zero when a finding is at or above `--fail-on`.
  </Step>
</Steps>
