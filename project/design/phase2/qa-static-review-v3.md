# QA static review v3 · Phase 2 Timetable boards

**Author:** A11 (QA) · **Date:** 2026-11-14 · **Gate:** 2 (before the PO's review)
**Scope:** the 69 Phase 2 boards (pages `p2-entry` to `p2-states`) in `claude-design/timetable/canvas/project/`, the Timetable design system in `claude-design/timetable/ds/project/`, and the published copies of both. The 46 boards on the `archive` page are out of scope.
**Checked against:** `phase2/screen-specifications.md` (SS), `user-flows.md` (FL), `accessibility-spec.md` (A11Y), `golden-files.md` §15 (GF), `information-architecture.md` §5.4 (IA), `../realistic-data.md` / `.json` (RD), decision 35 (`phase2-decisions-pending.md`), decision 38 (PV-1 to PV-14, as summarised in ADR-010 §11 and `schema.md`/`contracts.md` of the p4-architecture worktree; the private A09 report was not read), and `scenarios-and-judge.md` (SJ) where a severity was in doubt.
**Method:** static only. Board HTML parsed by script (text, ARIA, headings, holes, imports, glyph use), values cross-checked against RD and the specs by script and by hand, contrast recomputed from `tokens.css`, published files compared with the local sources. I did not rely on the author's summary. Nothing was published, committed or changed apart from this file.

## Verdict

**READY WITH FIXES.** There are no Blockers: the boards are complete enough for the PO to judge the direction, Run #3 is reproduced faithfully, and the published artifacts match the local sources. 13 Majors should be fixed before Gate 2 sign-off and the A07 handoff. Most are concentrated and cheap: two generator helpers (`banner()`, `pathstrip()`), one draft table, missing `h1`s, ARIA roles, a few states that aren't drawn, and the PV-related copy and states, which need A03's wording first.
